# Project Final Closeout

## Purpose

Close project obligations and preserve an operable, auditable handover rather than merely declaring the schedule finished.

## Inputs

- charter/scope, contracts or obligations, milestones, and acceptance criteria
- final decision, risk, issue, change, and acceptance registers
- final deliverables and version baseline
- handover, training, operations, warranty, finance/procurement, and archive requirements

## Workflow

1. Freeze the closeout cutoff and inventory expected versus actual deliverables.
2. Verify final acceptance and every conditional obligation; separate closed, waived, transferred, residual, and disputed items.
3. Build the final artifact/version/source-of-truth index.
4. Reconcile decision, risk, issue, change, contract/procurement, and acceptance logs.
5. Verify operational readiness: owner, access, SOP, training, support, warranty, maintenance, rollback/contingency, and known limitations.
6. Create a residual register with owner, absolute date, acceptor, control, escalation, and expiry.
7. Capture outcome, variances, lessons, reusable workflows, and career-safe evidence.
8. Obtain authorized sign-off and record archive location and retention boundary.

## Rules

- Do not label a project closed because a milestone date passed.
- Do not hide residuals inside narrative notes.
- Never invent approval, payment, warranty, legal, safety, or contractual status.
- Distinguish project closeout from commercial/contract closeout when evidence differs.
- Redact confidential or personal content from reusable/career assets.

## Output

- closeout decision and sign-off matrix
- deliverable/version inventory
- residual and dispute register
- operational handover checklist
- reconciled log status
- archive index
- outcome/variance/lessons summary
- post-close review date

## Boundary and Failure Handling

AI audits completeness and drafts closeout artifacts. Authorized owners sign acceptance, finance, legal, compliance, and operational transfer. If mandatory evidence is absent, return `NOT READY FOR CLOSEOUT` with exact missing items and responsible owners.

## Completion Check

All obligations are closed, explicitly waived, disputed, or bounded as residuals; operation has accepted ownership; versions and evidence are findable; required sign-offs exist.
